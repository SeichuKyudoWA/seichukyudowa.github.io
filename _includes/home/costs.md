## Costs
- Fee: $35 per practice, 3 hours.
- Membership: $100 / year. Existing Members collected annually, June 1. New members, after 1 month of classes, prorated.
- We do not offer "drop in shooting" for non members, however visitors from other IKYF clubs are allowed.
- Note: Starting in Spring 2027 classes will be 4 hours long:  9AM-1PM.
